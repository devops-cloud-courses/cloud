### Cloud Migration Pathways (The "R"s)

<img src="img/cloud-migration.svg" alt="Cloud Migration Strategies: Retain, Retire, Rehost, Redeploy, Replatform, Refactor, Rebuild, Repurchase" />

Note:
* Choosing the right migration pathway balances transformation effort against expected business agility and value.
* Keep the lights on: Retire (decommission) or Retain (keep on-premises).
* IaaS (Lift & Shift): Rehost (move as-is) or Redeploy (reinstall on cloud VMs with minor OS/middleware updates).
* PaaS (App Modernization): Replatform (adopt managed DBs/containers with minimal code changes), Refactor (re-architect for cloud-native), or Rebuild (rewrite from scratch using Serverless/microservices).
* SaaS (Packaged Software): Repurchase (replace custom or legacy software with a turnkey SaaS solution).
